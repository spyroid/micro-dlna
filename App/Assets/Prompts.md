# Icon generation prompts

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool.

Assets: [app PNG](AppIcon.png), [app ICNS](AppIcon.icns), and
[menu bar source PNG](MenuBarIcon-source.png). The menu bar icon is bundled
at 18px and 36px and rendered as a macOS template image.

## App icon

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production macOS desktop application icon for μDLNA server, a tiny local home media sharing app. Create ONE finished square icon, centered, front-facing, no mockup or presentation sheet. Design: a deep indigo to cobalt blue rounded-square macOS app tile with a subtle refined satin dimensional finish. Inside the tile a bold simple white rounded television/screen outline with one large beautifully drawn lowercase Greek letter mu 'μ' centered on the screen. The mu must be unmistakably Greek lowercase μ, with a descender, not a Latin u or a play triangle. Very restrained subtle cool light highlights, calm polished native desktop aesthetic, crisp geometry, large shapes, generous padding, excellent legibility at 32px. Screen outline and mu form one balanced emblem. Tile occupies about 86% of the square canvas width. Transparent background outside the rounded tile, including corners. No words, no other letters, no wifi symbol, no antenna, no extra badges, no watermark, no scenery, no surrounding frame. High resolution square raster, preferably 1024 by 1024.

## Thin monitor outline

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: the provided blue μDLNA application icon. Change ONLY the thickness of the white rounded monitor/screen outline: its stroke must be exactly half the thickness of the main vertical stroke of the white Greek lowercase μ. Thus the monitor outline is a distinctly finer line, while the μ stays bold and dominant, especially when viewed as a tiny menu bar icon. Keep the monitor outline outer dimensions, overall location, corner curvature, and the Greek μ's exact shape, thickness, scale and position unchanged. Preserve the blue rounded-square tile, lighting, colors, material, stand, composition and padding. Preserve genuine transparency outside the rounded tile. Do not add or remove other elements. One finished square icon, no mockup, no text or watermark.

## Menu bar icon

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: monochrome macOS menu bar template icon on a genuinely transparent background. Reference image: blue μDLNA icon. Extract and simplify ONLY its central monitor-with-Greek-mu emblem into flat solid BLACK strokes on transparent pixels. Remove the blue tile and all gradients, lighting, shadows, highlights and 3D material entirely. Keep the recognizable rounded monitor shape with its small centered stand and the bold lowercase Greek μ inside. The screen bezel line thickness MUST be one-half of the μ's main stroke thickness (for example μ stroke 80 units, bezel stroke 40 units); not thinner than that. Keep the μ unmistakably Greek lowercase mu with left descender and right curved foot, not a Latin u. The letter is the dominant element. Use clean crisp rounded geometry; no texture and no color. Center the emblem and scale it to occupy about 90 percent of the square canvas width with a small safe margin. Design specifically to remain readable at 18 by 18 points. All visible foreground pixels should be black; background and monitor interior fully transparent, with antialiasing only at shape edges. One symbol only, no enclosing rounded-square tile, no captions, no mockup, no other elements, no watermark.

## Menu bar outline refinement

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: the provided flat black monitor with Greek μ on transparent background. Make ONLY the rounded monitor bezel/frame stroke 50 percent THICKER than it is now. Keep the large Greek μ exactly unchanged. The desired final monitor frame line width is HALF of the thickness of the μ's main left vertical stroke: if μ thickness is 90 pixels, frame thickness must be 45 pixels, not 25 or 30 pixels. Preserve the frame centerline and corner shapes, overall layout, monitor stand and letter geometry. Flat pure black monochrome template icon, transparent background including inside the monitor. No tile, no white fill, no shading, no texture, no extra objects, no added text. Output a single finished square transparent icon.
